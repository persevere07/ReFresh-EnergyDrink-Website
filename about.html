Our Story

About Re-Fresh

We're on a mission to revolutionize the energy drink industry with clean, sustainable energy that actually works.

> Our Story

Re-Fresh was born from a simple frustration: why do energy drinks have to be loaded with sugar and artificial ingredients, only to leave you crashing later in the day?

We set out to create something different—a clean energy drink designed to power your day without unnecessary compromise.

Founded in Huddersfield, UK, we're a team of health-conscious creators, athletes, and professionals who believe energy should enhance your performance, not derail it.

Every can of Re-Fresh is crafted with carefully selected ingredients designed to provide sustained focus and energy.

> Our Mission

Our mission is to provide clean, sustainable energy that fits into your lifestyle without unnecessary sugar spikes, artificial additives, or energy crashes.

We're committed to transparency, quality, and helping you perform at your best—whether you're in the office, at the gym, or wherever your day takes you.

> What Makes Us Different

  • Zero Sugar: No added sugar, with a focus on clean and refreshing flavor.
  • Clean Caffeine: 160mg of caffeine from green coffee beans for sustained energy.
  • Electrolytes: Designed to complement your active lifestyle.
  • B-Vitamins: Includes B-vitamins as part of our performance-focused formula.
  • Vegan & Gluten-Free: Made with dietary preferences in mind.

> Our Values

At Re-Fresh, we believe in doing things the right way. That means focusing on quality ingredients, transparent labeling, responsible practices, and a commitment to helping our customers perform at their best.

We're not just another energy drink. We're building a brand centered around better choices, better performance, and a fresher approach to everyday energy.
