Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Re-Fresh.

Is Re-Fresh sugar free? +

Yes. Re-Fresh contains zero sugar and only 10 calories per can. We use a light, modern sweetness designed to keep the flavor crisp without making it overly sweet.

How much caffeine is in a can? +

Each can contains 160mg of caffeine. Re-Fresh is designed to provide focused energy while keeping the experience smooth and refreshing.

Can I drink more than one can a day? +

We recommend starting with one can and staying within the caffeine limits appropriate for you. Individual caffeine tolerance varies, so always consider your total daily caffeine intake from all sources.

Is Re-Fresh suitable for late-night work? +

Re-Fresh is designed for focused activities such as studying, coding, gaming, and creative work. However, caffeine can affect sleep, so consider your personal tolerance and the time of day before consuming it.

Where do you ship? +

This website is currently a front-end demo. For a production launch, shipping regions and delivery options can be connected to an e-commerce platform such as Shopify or Stripe.
